get to know me

Developer by day,
musician by night.

Hi, I'm Sanka Darshana — a software engineer based in Sri Lanka with a deep passion for building systems that are both blazingly fast and genuinely useful. I specialise in backend development using Rust, working across blockchain protocols and FinTech infrastructure.

Outside of code, I wear a completely different hat. I write and produce Sri Lankan music rooted in our culture, alongside English pop songs that I hope connect with listeners around the world. Music is my way of expressing what code can't.

I believe the most interesting people stand at the intersection of logic and creativity — and that's the space I try to live in every day.
